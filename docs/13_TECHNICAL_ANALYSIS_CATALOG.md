# Technical Analysis Catalog and Calculation Boundary

## Purpose

The Technical Analysis page makes the supplied trading-analysis master list searchable and gives every entry an explicit coverage label. It is a taxonomy and research surface; listing a concept does not mean the application can detect it, measure it correctly on every market, or turn it into an edge.

The catalog is organized into nine top-level families:

1. SMC & ICT — market structure, liquidity, blocks, imbalance/price delivery, premium-discount/Fibonacci, time/session concepts, and Model & Setup ICT.
2. Price Action — market conditions, candlestick patterns, and chart patterns.
3. Support, Resistance & Trend — levels, channels, pitchfork/median-line methods, role reversal, and confluence.
4. Indikator Teknikal — trend, momentum, volatility, and volume indicators.
5. Order Flow & Market Profile — auction concepts, profile levels, order-book/footprint features, absorption, and exhaustion.
6. Divergence & Intermarket Analysis — regular/hidden divergence, relative strength, correlation, and lead-lag relationships.
7. Metode Analisis Lain — Wyckoff, Elliott Wave, harmonic patterns, and other theories/approaches.
8. Fundamental & Makro — central-bank policy, inflation/jobs/GDP, yields, dollar strength, gold flows, event risk, and slippage context.
9. Risk Management & Evaluation — execution, sizing, exposure constraints, performance metrics, and validation methods.

The exact item names and groups used by the UI live in `src/lib/technical-analysis.js`; the strict ordered inventory snapshot is `scripts/fixtures/technical-analysis-master-list.v1.json`. The snapshot is checked item-for-item, including category, subgroup, label, and implementation status.

## Calculators available in v0.1.7

All readings are calculated in the frontend from the currently loaded OHLCV candle series. They do not currently have independent symbol/timeframe selection, persistent indicator configuration, multi-timeframe alignment, or server-side provenance per reading.

| Reading | Calculation in this version | Important limit |
|---|---|---|
| SMA 9 / 21 | Arithmetic average of the latest 9 and 21 closes | Averages alone do not constitute a crossover event or entry signal. |
| EMA 20 | Exponential smoothing with alpha `2 / (period + 1)`, seeded from the first 20-close mean | Uses the loaded sample as its seed; longer history can change the reading. |
| RSI 14 | Wilder-smoothed average gain/loss ratio | Conventional 30/70 levels are references, not guarantees or standalone triggers. |
| MACD 12 / 26 / 9 | EMA(12) minus EMA(26), with EMA(9) signal and histogram | Needs at least 34 closes for this implementation. |
| ATR 14 | Wilder-smoothed true range | Measures range/volatility, not direction. Requires 15 candles here. |
| Bollinger Bands 20 / 2σ | 20-close mean ± two population standard deviations | Band touches are not standalone reversal or breakout signals. |

Missing history and malformed OHLCV mark all readings unavailable. The browser runtime is disabled for market-dependent work. Technical readings are unavailable unless the native application supplies a validated candle snapshot. Tests exercise the formulas' baseline behaviors and failure cases, not all market edge cases.

## Coverage labels

- **Calculated now:** the six calculator families above.
- **Workflow available:** backtesting exists as a separate SMA-crossover-only runner. It does not mean a catalog concept is used in that strategy.
- **Catalog only:** indexed and searchable, but no detector/calculator/tested implementation is present in v0.1.7.

There is deliberately no generic “bullish” or “bearish” decision generated by this page, no automated orders, and no change to the Rust risk gateway. It is not an investment recommendation or a validated signal service.

## Strict inventory contract (v1.0.0)

The application currently enforces exactly **364 taxonomy entries** in the nine category labels exactly as provided by the requester. Expected category counts are pinned in code and tested against the reviewed JSON snapshot. `SMT Divergence` is intentionally cross-listed in SMC & ICT and Divergence & Intermarket Analysis, so there are 364 contextual entries and 363 unique displayed names. Catalog order is also contractual to make regressions reviewable.

Coverage status is not collapsed into a generic availability badge: six families are **Calculated now**, the existing SMA-crossover **Backtesting** entry is **Workflow available**, and 357 entries are **Catalog only**. Changing the inventory requires an intentional manifest update and a corresponding review; a larger or smaller count does not silently pass.

## Next implementation slices

1. Persist symbol, interval, and exact candle range alongside every analysis result; unify current chart/backtest/readings data selection.
2. Extend deterministic indicator implementations with independent known-vector tests and numeric edge-case coverage; prioritize volume-aware indicators only when their input data contract exists.
3. Add market-structure primitives (swing points, BOS/CHoCH) only after pivot confirmation and no-look-ahead semantics are specified.
4. Add pattern-specific fixture sets, uncertainty/invalid states, and benchmark tests before changing any pattern status from catalog-only.
5. For session/macro concepts, require timezone/calendar and source provenance instead of deriving them from incomplete candle data.

The release gate remains unchanged: the full Svelte build, Rust test/Clippy suite, lockfiles, and cross-platform packaging must pass before release readiness can be claimed. Live trading stays disabled.
